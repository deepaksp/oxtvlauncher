Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

OxTV Launcher is designed with privacy in mind. OxTV is a TV launcher that helps you access applications and launcher features on your Android TV or compatible Android device.

We do not operate a service that collects or maintains a personal profile of our users. The information accessed by OxTV is processed locally on your device to provide launcher functionality.

1. Information We Access

OxTV may access certain information on your device because this information is necessary for the launcher and related features to work. This may include:

  • Information about applications installed on your device, such as application names, icons, and launchable activities.
  • Information about available TV applications and TV content when supported by your Android TV device.
  • TV listing, Watch Next, or related program information when the device provides access to this information.
  • Images and videos that you choose to access for launcher backgrounds, wallpapers, or related features.
  • Launcher preferences and settings required to remember your configuration.

OxTV does not use this information to create a personal profile about you or to sell it. It is stored locally on your device.

2. Installed Applications

As a launcher, OxTV needs to discover applications installed on your device so that it can display them and allow you to launch them.

Information about installed applications may include application names, package information, icons, and available launchable activities.

This application information is used for launcher functionality, including displaying applications, organizing applications, creating favorites, and launching applications.

This information is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to OxTV servers for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

3. TV Listings and Watch Next Data

On compatible Android TV devices, OxTV may access TV listing, Watch Next, recommended, or related program information provided by the Android TV system.

This information is used to display TV-related content and launcher features on your device.

TV listing information accessed through the device is processed locally by OxTV and is not transmitted to OxTV servers.

Availability of these features depends on the Android TV device, system software, and permissions provided by the device manufacturer.

4. Accessibility Service

OxTV may provide an Accessibility Service that you can enable through Android system settings.

The Accessibility Service is used to help OxTV detect which launcher or application is currently in the foreground and to provide launcher override or related launcher functionality.

OxTV does not use the Accessibility Service to read, collect, sell, or transmit passwords, messages, personal communications, or other unrelated user content.

Accessibility information is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to OxTV servers.

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time through the Android system accessibility settings.

5. Images, Videos, and Device Storage

OxTV allows you to select images and videos from your device for features such as wallpapers, backgrounds, and other launcher customization.

OxTV accesses these files only when required for a feature you choose to use.

Your selected images and videos are not uploaded to OxTV servers. They remain on your device unless you choose to use another Android feature or application to share or transfer them.

OxTV may also provide local file-management or backup functionality. Any files accessed through these features are handled according to the action you initiate on your device.

6. Network Access and Remote Content

OxTV requires network access for certain features and may request content from internet URLs, such as remote images, banners, or other content configured by the user.

When OxTV connects directly to an external URL, the request may be received by the server hosting that URL. The external server may process standard technical information associated with a network request, such as an IP address, according to that provider's own privacy policy.

OxTV does not use these network requests to create or maintain a personal advertising profile.

External content is controlled by the respective third-party provider, and their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

7. App Installation and Uninstallation

OxTV may provide functionality that allows you to initiate application installation or uninstallation actions using Android's system mechanisms.

These actions are initiated by you and are handled by Android's package-management system. OxTV does not use application-management information for advertising or user profiling.

8. Premium Features and Google Play Billing

Some OxTV features may require a purchase through Google Play.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. OxTV does not receive or store your credit card number, debit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google may process purchase and transaction information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

9. Information We Do Not Collect

OxTV does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your postal address
  • Your contacts
  • Your messages
  • Your precise location
  • Your advertising profile

OxTV does not intentionally collect analytics or advertising data about your use of the launcher.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal or sensitive user data with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

Data accessed locally by OxTV remains on your device unless a feature explicitly involves communication with an external service, such as loading content from a URL or processing a purchase through Google Play.

External services involved in such features operate under their own privacy policies.

11. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

OxTV does not operate a user account system or a server-side database containing personal user profiles.

Launcher settings, preferences, and other locally required data may remain on your device for as long as they are needed by the app.

You can remove locally stored OxTV data by using the Android system's app storage controls or by uninstalling OxTV, subject to Android's own system behavior.

Because OxTV does not maintain user accounts or a personal user database, there is no OxTV account requiring account deletion.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the handling of user data to what is necessary for OxTV's functionality.

Data that remains on your device is protected by the security mechanisms provided by Android and your device manufacturer.

Where OxTV communicates with external services, secure communication such as HTTPS is used where supported by the service.

13. Third-Party Libraries and Services

OxTV may use Android, AndroidX, Google Play Billing, and other software libraries required to provide application functionality.

Third-party libraries are not permitted to use OxTV data for purposes inconsistent with this Privacy Policy.

Services operated by third parties, including Google Play, may process information according to their own privacy policies.

14. Children's Privacy

OxTV does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core launcher functionality.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when OxTV's functionality, services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page, together with the date it was last updated.
